E-COMMERCE SALES ANALYSIS – BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. OVERALL PERFORMANCE

Total Sales      : $5,296,907.13
Total Profit     : $534,110.89
Total Orders     : 3,500
Average Order Value : $1,513.40

The business generated approximately $5.30 million in sales
and $534K in profit from 3,500 orders.

Approximate Profit Margin: 10.08%


2. CATEGORY INSIGHT

Technology is the highest-selling category.

Technology Sales: $3,447,751.75

Insight:
Technology is the strongest category and is the main contributor
to overall revenue.

Recommendation:
Focus on high-performing Technology products and maintain
sufficient inventory for these products.


3. REGIONAL SALES INSIGHT

Central Region : $1,377,785.78
West Region    : $1,336,727.86
East Region    : $1,333,694.80
South Region   : $1,248,698.69

Insight:
Central is the highest-performing region by sales.

The South region has the lowest sales among the four regions.

Recommendation:
Continue strong business activities in Central while investigating
ways to improve sales performance in the South region.


4. MONTHLY SALES INSIGHT

Best Year-Month:
December 2023 = $355,100.78

Best Calendar Month:
December = $843,468.20

Insight:
December is the strongest sales month in the analysis.

Recommendation:
The business should prepare inventory and marketing campaigns
before the December period to take advantage of higher demand.


5. PRODUCT INSIGHT

The Top 10 products by sales are mainly laptop products.

Insight:
Laptop-related products are important revenue-generating
products in the dataset.

Recommendation:
Focus on inventory planning and marketing for high-performing
laptop products.


6. DISCOUNT AND PROFIT INSIGHT

Discount-Profit Correlation = -0.416

Insight:
There is a negative correlation between discount and profit.
Higher discounts tend to be associated with lower profit in
this dataset.

Important:
Correlation does not prove that discounts directly cause lower
profit.

Recommendation:
The business should carefully control high discount levels
and analyze profit margins before offering large discounts.


7. KEY BUSINESS FINDINGS

1. Technology is the strongest sales category.
2. Central is the highest-performing region by sales.
3. Central also has the highest total profit.
4. December is the strongest calendar month.
5. Laptop products dominate the top-selling product list.
6. Higher discounts are associated with lower profit.
7. South is the lowest-performing region by sales.


8. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Focus on high-performing Technology products.
2. Maintain sufficient inventory for popular laptop products.
3. Prepare additional inventory before December.
4. Increase marketing during high-demand periods.
5. Investigate ways to improve sales in the South region.
6. Avoid unnecessarily high discount levels.
7. Monitor profit margins when creating promotional offers.
8. Use state-level analysis to identify strong and weak markets.


9. PROJECT CONCLUSION

The E-Commerce Sales Analysis shows that the business generated
$5.30 million in sales and $534K in profit across 3,500 orders.

Technology was the strongest category, while Central was the
strongest region. December showed the highest overall monthly
sales performance. Laptop products were prominent among the
top-selling products.

The negative discount-profit correlation of -0.416 suggests
that higher discounts are associated with lower profitability.

Overall, the business should focus on high-performing products
and regions, prepare for seasonal demand, improve weaker markets,
and carefully manage discounts to protect profitability.
